Help/Trust and safety/AI can make mistakes

Aviqo AI can make mistakes. What should you do?

Updated October 28, 2026 · 6 min read

Aviqo is a fast AI assistant, but it does not always get things right. This article explains why mistakes happen, where to be careful, and how to check an answer before you rely on it.

Why mistakes happen

Aviqo writes answers by predicting what fits best. It does not look up every fact in a verified database, so it can:

  • Sound confident while being wrong.
  • Miss the latest news, prices, or rules, because its knowledge has a cutoff date.
  • Make errors in calculations, long lists, and step-by-step math.
  • Misread text in a photo, especially if it is blurry, small, or cropped.
  • Invent a name, link, or source that does not exist.
  • Mix up two similar things, such as two products, laws, or people with the same name.
Rule of thumb: treat Aviqo's answer as a strong first draft, not the final word. Be extra careful when money, health, law, exams, or safety are involved.

Where to be extra careful

Money and payments

Prices, bills, loan terms, and investment decisions.

Health and medicine

Doses and symptoms. Confirm with a doctor or pharmacist.

Legal matters

Contracts, notices, and rights. Check with a qualified lawyer.

Exams and homework

Work the steps yourself, then compare with the answer.

Code before launch

Test code with sample data before using it for real.

News and dates

Check an official source for anything time-sensitive.

How to check an answer

  1. Check important numbers and names against an official website or document.
  2. Ask a follow-up. Try "Show the steps" or "Are you sure? What could be wrong?" This often shows a weak point.
  3. Compare with a second source for anything you will act on.
  4. Look at the original photo yourself for key details Aviqo reads from it.
  5. Test code before you use it in a live project.
  6. Give it context. Mention your country, date, and goal. Vague questions get vague answers.

Examples of a good check

  • Math: redo the last step on a calculator. If the final number is off, ask Aviqo to show each step.
  • Prices or rules: open the official site of the shop, bank, or government department.
  • A quote or a link: search the exact title. If you cannot find it, treat it as unverified.

Keep your information safe

Do not share passwords, OTPs, bank card numbers, or other sensitive personal details in a chat. Share only what you need to get help.

Report a wrong answer

Use the thumbs-down button under any answer. Your feedback is saved for our team to review, so we can improve Aviqo over time. Thumbs-up tells us what works.

Release notes

What has changed in Aviqo recently.

Latest updates

New features and fixes are described here as they launch. Reload the chat page after an update to see the latest version.

What can I use Aviqo for?

Common things people do with Aviqo.

  • Ask quick questions and get clear explanations in your own language.
  • Write and improve emails, posts, captions, and documents.
  • Study with step-by-step solutions, notes, and practice questions.
  • Generate images and short videos from a description.
  • Ask questions about a photo you upload.
  • Plan projects and build simple websites or apps.

Where can I access Aviqo?

Aviqo works in your web browser.

Open the Aviqo website and start the chat page. Aviqo works on a computer or a phone, in a current browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

How up-to-date is Aviqo's knowledge?

Aviqo learns from information collected up to a cutoff date.

Because of this cutoff, Aviqo may not know very recent news, prices, offers, or rules. Chat answers do not browse the internet live, so for current information check an official website or a trusted source. Aviqo can still help you understand the topic and plan what to check.

What interfaces can I use to access Aviqo?

Ways to talk to Aviqo.

  • Chat page: the full conversation view with history, buttons, and the + menu.
  • Home page chatbox: type a message on the home page and it opens in the chat page.
  • Phone browser: the same chat page works on a phone.

Verify your email

Confirm your email after you sign up.

After sign-up, the sidebar may show Check email to verify. Open the email from Aviqo and follow the link. If you cannot find the email, check your spam or promotions folder. If it still does not arrive, sign up again with the correct email address.

Conversation management

Start new chats, find old ones, and remove them.

New chats and recents

Click New chat in the sidebar to start a fresh conversation. Your earlier chats appear under Recents. Click one to open it again.

Delete a chat

Hover over a chat under Recents and click the ✕ to remove it. Deleted chats are removed from this browser.

Chat history

Chats are saved in your browser, separately for each signed-in email. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Getting started

Everything you need for your first conversation.

Your first chat

Open the chat page and type your question in the box at the bottom. Press Enter or the arrow button to send it. Aviqo replies in the same language you use, including Hindi, Hinglish, and English.

What Aviqo can do

Aviqo can answer questions, write and explain content, help with study topics, generate images and videos, and build simple websites and apps. You can see the full list on the home page.

Chat

How the conversation works, and the buttons under each answer.

New chat and recent chats

Click New chat in the sidebar to start fresh. Your earlier chats appear under Recents. Click one to open it again.

Buttons under an answer

  • Copy: copies the answer to your clipboard.
  • Thumbs up / down: rates the answer. Thumbs-down is saved for review.
  • Read aloud: speaks the answer in clean words. Click again to stop.
  • Share: copy a link, or share to X, LinkedIn, or Reddit. A shared link shows that one answer.
  • Regenerate: asks again for a fresh answer to your last question.

Formatting

Answers can include headings, bullet points, tables, code blocks with a Copy button, and math formulas.

Images and video

Create visuals, or ask questions about a photo you upload.

Generate an image

Type a clear request, such as "generate an image of a futuristic city at night." Image generation can take a little time. The image opens in a new tab as well.

Create a video

Video is a slow job. It usually takes 30 to 90 seconds. Keep the chat open until the video appears. If it takes longer than expected, try again later.

Ask about a photo

Use the + button, then Upload or add photo, or press Ctrl+U. Aviqo can describe the photo, read visible text, explain a chart, or help with a question shown in an image. Use a clear, well-lit photo for the best result.

Websites and apps

Ask Aviqo to build a simple website or app for you.

Build a website

Tell Aviqo what the site is for, who it is for, and what sections you want, such as Home, About, and Contact. Use the + menu, then Build a website, for a quick start.

Build an app

Describe the main job of the app and the key screens. Start small, such as a simple expense tracker, and add features one at a time.

Prompting tips

  • Say the goal, the audience, and the language you want the text in.
  • Ask for one step at a time when the project is big.
  • Test the output yourself before you publish it.

Study help

Learn faster with step-by-step explanations.

Step-by-step solutions

Ask for the steps, not only the answer, for example: "Solve this and explain each step." Then check the steps against your textbook.

Notes and study plans

Ask for short notes on a topic, a 7-day revision plan, or practice questions with answers. Ask Aviqo to quiz you after an explanation.

Exams

Use Aviqo to practise and understand. Do not rely on it as the only source for exam content. Check your syllabus and official study material.

Usage and limits

How much you can use Aviqo, and what to do when it is busy.

Fair use

When Aviqo is busy

AI services can be busy at peak times. If you see "Aviqo is very busy right now," wait a minute and try again. Your earlier chat stays saved.

Privacy and legal

What is stored, and what you should share.

What is stored

  • Chat history: saved in your browser so you can see it again.
  • Feedback: thumbs-up and thumbs-down are sent to our server so the team can review them.

What is sent to the AI

Your messages and any photo you attach are sent to an AI service to produce an answer. Do not send sensitive information you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

Terms and policies

For terms, privacy, and other legal questions, use the links on the home page or contact us through the Team page.

Safeguards

What Aviqo will not help with, and how we keep answers safer.

What Aviqo will not do

Aviqo will not help with requests that are harmful, illegal, or that involve abuse, fraud, or dangerous instructions. It may also decline to write things that impersonate a real person or organisation.

Safer answers

For health, money, and legal questions, Aviqo gives general information and suggests a qualified professional. For sensitive topics, you may get a short answer with a safety note.

If an answer seems wrong or unsafe

Use thumbs-down and, if you can, write what was wrong in your next message. This helps the team improve.

Troubleshooting

Common messages and what to do.

"Looks like there's a connection issue"

Your device could not reach Aviqo. Check your internet connection, then send the message again.

"Aviqo is very busy right now"

The AI service is busy. Wait a minute and try again.

"Could not save your feedback"

The feedback did not reach the server. Check your connection and try again.

Read aloud has no sound

Check your device volume and that the browser is not muted. Some browsers have fewer voices, so the sound may differ from device to device.

A shared link shows only one answer

This is expected. A shared link opens one answer. To start your own conversation, use the link at the top of that page.

Still need help?

If none of the topics above solve your problem, reach out through the Team page. Include what you were doing and the message you saw.
