AI Disclaimer

AI can make mistakes. Outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete or misleading. Always verify important information before you act on it.

Accuracy

Text, images, video, code, websites and apps are produced by probabilistic models. They can contain factual errors, fabricated statements or references (“hallucinations”), outdated information, or bias.

Not professional advice

Outputs are for general informational and creative use only. They are not legal, medical, financial, tax or other professional advice. Consult a qualified professional before making decisions.

Your responsibility

You are solely responsible for reviewing, editing and verifying generated content, and for how you use, publish or share it, including compliance with applicable laws and third-party rights.

Similarity & ownership

Similar or identical outputs may be generated for other users. We do not guarantee that outputs are unique or free from third-party intellectual property claims.

Sensitive information

Avoid entering confidential, sensitive personal or regulated information in prompts. Never rely on Aviqo AI for emergencies or safety-critical decisions.

Third-party models

Some features may use third-party AI models and services. Their availability and behaviour can change without notice.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Aviqo AI, its directors, employees and affiliates are not liable for any loss, damage or claim arising from reliance on AI-generated output. This disclaimer should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

02 / BILLING

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Simple, transparent billing for Aviqo AI subscriptions and credit packs.

Cancel anytimeFrom your account or by email
7-day windowFor first-time paid plan purchases
5–7 business daysTo credit approved refunds
Cancellation
  • You may cancel your paid plan at any time from your Account page or by emailing us.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. You keep access to paid features until the end of the current billing period.
  • We do not provide pro-rata refunds for the unused part of a billing period, except as described below.
When a refund is available
  • You request a refund within 7 days of your first purchase of a paid plan and have used only a small portion of the included credits.
  • You were charged more than once for the same plan, or charged an incorrect amount because of a technical error.
  • A paid feature was materially unavailable for an extended period because of a fault on our side that we could not resolve.
When a refund is not available
  • Renewals where the plan was not cancelled before the renewal date, unless you contact us within 48 hours of the charge and have not used the renewed credits.
  • Credits that have already been consumed, and credit packs or top-ups after use.
  • Promotional or discounted purchases, unless the offer states otherwise.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violating our Terms & Conditions.
  • Dissatisfaction with a specific AI output alone. Please write to us and we will do our best to help.
How to request a refund

Email hello@aviqoai.com with the subject “Refund Request” and include your registered email address, transaction or order ID, date of payment and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Processing time & failed payments
  • Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval. Bank and payment gateway timelines may add to this.
  • If money was deducted but your payment failed, it is normally reversed automatically by your bank or the payment gateway within 5–7 business days. If it is not, contact us with your transaction ID.
03 / ETHICS

Responsible AI & Ethics Policy

We are building AI that people can trust: safe, fair, transparent and accountable.

Safety first

We design safeguards to reduce harmful, illegal or abusive outputs, and we keep improving them as risks evolve.

Fairness & inclusion

We work to reduce bias in how our AI responds and to serve people across languages, regions and backgrounds.

Transparency

We are clear that you are interacting with AI, and we are honest about its limits, as set out in our AI Disclaimer.

Privacy & data protection

We collect only the data needed to run the service and handle it as described in our Privacy Policy.

Human oversight

People remain accountable for how AI is built and used. High-impact decisions should always involve human review.

Accountability

We review reported issues, act on misuse, and welcome feedback to help us hold ourselves to these principles.

Prohibited uses

You must not use Aviqo AI to create, request or distribute any of the following. We may restrict or suspend accounts that do.

  • Unlawful, fraudulent or deceptive content
  • Sexual content involving minors, or any exploitation of children
  • Harassment, hate speech or discrimination
  • Deepfakes or impersonation intended to mislead
  • Malware, hacking tools or cyber-attack assistance
  • Large-scale misinformation or election manipulation
  • Content that infringes privacy or intellectual property rights
  • Fully automated decisions with legal or health impact, without human review

To report a safety or ethics concern, write to hello@aviqoai.com or use the Grievance Officer details below.
