About Aviqo AI

One team, one belief — AI should feel like one tool, not ten.

Aviqo AI started with a simple frustration: creating anything online meant juggling a different app for every step — one tool for images, another for video, another for the website, another just to keep track of tasks. We built Aviqo to collapse all of that into a single place.

Our Mission

To give every individual and small business the power of a full creative and technical team — inside one AI, available anytime.

Our Vision

A future where you describe an outcome in plain language, and one trusted AI carries it from idea to finished product.

Where We Started

Aviqo AI is built and run out of Punjab, India — a small, focused team designing for creators and businesses everywhere.
